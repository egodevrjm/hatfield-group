Scotland rule

The Scotch is not ready. That is the point.

Hatfield Perthshire Single Malt began filling barrels in 2025. The earliest legal Scotch release would be 2028, but Fiona MacAllister is building a longer maturation programme. The current commercial product is Hatfield Highland Gin.

Bardstown

Carolyn Hatfield-Moore leads the bourbon programme: Original, Small Batch, Single Barrel, Reserve, Cave Select, rye, wheated, bonded, and experimental malt whiskey.

Perthshire

Fiona MacAllister and Rory Irvine run the Scotland programme: gin in market, new make in barrel, Still House Scotland on the grounds.
