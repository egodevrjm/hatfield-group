Mandate / 002

Ownership is a responsibility before it is an opportunity.

WW–PUB

Publishing

Composition rights, registrations, royalties, metadata, disputes and carefully judged synchronisation. Context and permanence matter alongside price.

WW–REC

Recordings

Master ownership, high-quality distribution, approved reissues, restoration, neighbouring rights and accurate accounting. Preservation masters remain protected.

WW–ARC

Archive

Unreleased audio, film, photography, correspondence and production records are conserved under logged, purpose-limited access. Ownership alone does not settle the ethical question.

WW–HWA

Homer Wilson Rights

Production royalties, participation accounting, legacy contracts, credit accuracy and approved uses of Homer Wilson’s name and production history.

Standard / 001

The decision record matters.

  1. 01

    Preserve optionality

    There is no duty to monetise everything that can be monetised. Silence can be a valid decision.

  2. 02

    Document context

    Every approval records what was proposed, what was decided and why future stewards should understand it.

  3. 03

    Answer clearly

    Every serious request receives an answer, including when that answer is no.

Enquiries / 001

Begin with the work, the context and the rights required.

Licence and research enquiries should identify the intended use, term, territory, distribution and editorial setting. Access to archive material is never implied by an enquiry.
