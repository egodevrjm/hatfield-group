Accessibility

Walker Holdings aims to make this website clear, direct and usable by the widest practical audience.

Our approach

The site uses semantic structure, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, responsive text and reduced-motion support. It is designed to remain legible without images or custom web fonts.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier, email office@walkerholdings.example with the page and issue. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information in an accessible form and correct the underlying problem.
