The Rooms Between Notes

Recording rooms and the people who hear them.

Feature documentary · 94 min · 2023
PMO001▶   01:00:23:16SR 25

Synopsis

An observational journey through legendary rooms and improvised home setups, listening to engineers, musicians and caretakers explain how space changes performance. The film treats rooms as collaborators: containers for accident, ritual, labour and memory.

A Phoenix Media productionDirected by Mara Bell

The work

Recording rooms and the people who hear them.

Production notes

Filmed over three years across eight countries with a small crew. Long takes and production sound keep the rooms present rather than turning them into décor.
