Hands on Glass

Independent instrument makers describe what the hand knows.

Documentary podcast · 8 × 34 min · 2024
PMO003▶   01:00:23:16SR 25

Synopsis

A tactile audio series about glass harmonicas, bowed metal, handmade microphones and instruments that resist standardisation. Each episode begins with the sound before revealing the object and its maker.

A Phoenix Media productionDirected by Nia Harcourt

The work

Independent instrument makers describe what the hand knows.

Production notes

Binaural field recording and close-microphone workshops preserve the physical character of each build. The makers retain approval over trade-secret disclosure, not editorial conclusions.
