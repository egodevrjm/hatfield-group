Night Bus Radio

After midnight, a city tells the truth differently.

Limited series · 4 × 52 min · 2024
PMO002▶   01:00:23:16SR 25

Synopsis

Four cities, four overnight radio programmes and the listeners who use them as company. The series follows presenters, drivers, nurses, insomniacs and callers through the hours when public life becomes intimate.

A Phoenix Media productionDirected by Imani Lowe

The work

After midnight, a city tells the truth differently.

Production notes

Recorded largely in real time between midnight and dawn. Caller permissions and contributor welfare were built into the production schedule rather than treated as post-production paperwork.
