The Last Rehearsal Room

A working room disappears one rent review at a time.

Feature documentary · 102 min · 2025
PMO004▶   01:00:23:16SR 25

Synopsis

As a long-running London rehearsal complex prepares to close, seven bands, the building’s manager and a young promoter negotiate what is lost when creative infrastructure is valued only as floor area.

A Phoenix Media productionDirected by Leila Grant

The work

A working room disappears one rent review at a time.

Production notes

The production followed the final eighteen months without buying access or staging rehearsals. Planning documents and lease records ground the film’s larger argument about cultural space.
