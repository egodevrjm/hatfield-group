Company profile

Built from bourbon. Run like a real company.

Hatfield Group is a diversified spirits, hospitality, wine, beverage, provisions, licensing, and merchandise company headquartered in Louisville. Walker Holdings is the majority owner; David Buckner runs Hatfield day to day.

At a glance

Not a vanity brand. A serious operating company.

Founded1978

Rosie Walker acquired Hatfield Distillery after Homer Wilson became attached to the bourbon.

Hatfield has its own CEO, board, HR, finance, marketing, operations, and legal structure.

Leadership model

Clear ownership, expert operators, and room for each division to breathe.

David Buckner

Recruited in 2012 to professionalise the management structure, Buckner sets strategy, reviews P&L, visits locations, and lets expert operators do the work they were hired to do.

Canon rule

Alex knows Hatfield because it is family infrastructure. He does not run it.

The public can connect Alex Wilson to Hatfield, but the operating company remains professionally managed. Staff are warm, not deferential; family access is not an executive role.
